Welcome

The Mashup Builder turns songs you've already sequenced in xLights into a mashup: short sections of several songs played back to back, with the lights for each section carried over exactly as you sequenced them.

You choose the songs and which part of each to use — or let the app do it. When you build, you get a new xLights sequence (.xsq) and a matching music file (.mp3), ready to drop into your show.

  • Your show is never changed. The app only reads your sequences and music; everything it makes goes into its own output folder.
  • The lights come with the music. Every effect in the part you pick is copied across and moved to the right time — including videos, which are re-timed so the picture stays in sync.
  • It can do the hard parts for you. ✨ Automate finds good places to cut each song, and 🎲 Generate can pick the songs too.

Quick start

The fastest way to a finished mashup:

  1. Add some songs. In the library on the left, hover over a song and click + Add (or drag it onto the timeline). Add three to six songs.
  2. Click ✨ Automate at the bottom, then ✨ Analyze. The app listens to the songs, reads their lyrics, and suggests which part of each to use and in what order.
  3. Check the suggestion and click ✓ Apply to timeline. Fine-tune any clip with its slider if you like.
  4. Type a name in the Name box at the top and click 💾 Save so you can come back to it.
  5. Click 🔨 Build Mashup, then 📦 Download all (.zip).
  6. Unzip it into your xLights Sequences folder, open it in xLights and render. See Into xLights.
Don't want to choose songs at all? Click 🎲 Generate instead, pick a category like Christmas and how many songs you want, and the app picks and arranges them for you.

Sequence library

The left panel lists every sequence in your show's Sequences folder (copies inside import subfolders are skipped). Each card shows the folder, song name, length, number of effects and models, and its categories.

Finding songs

  • Search box — matches the song name, its folder, or its categories (try typing rock).
  • Category filter — the drop-down under the search box shows only songs in one category. The number in brackets is how many songs it has.

Adding songs to the mashup

  • Hover over a card and click + Add to put the song at the end of the timeline, or
  • drag the card onto the timeline and drop it exactly where you want it — a green line shows where it will land.

A song is added in full (start to end). Trim it afterwards, or let ✨ Automate choose the best part.

Fixing a song's categories

The automatic tags won't always be right. Hover over the song and click ✎ tags:

  • Your own categories (e.g. Disney, Party, Kids Night) appear in the filter and in 🎲 Generate under Your own.
  • Your changes are kept when the library is scanned again.
  • ↺ Back to automatic (shown once you've changed a song) undoes your changes.

Building the timeline

The timeline plays from top to bottom. Each card is one clip — the part of a song that will play in the mashup.

Trimming clips

  • Drag the green handle to set where the clip starts, and the red handle to set where it ends. The shaded bar shows the part of the song being used.
  • Or type a time into Start or End. These formats all work: 1:23.456, 1:23, 83.5 (seconds), or a plain number (90 = 90 seconds; numbers above 999 are read as milliseconds).
  • Click a handle and use the ← → arrow keys to nudge it in 10 ms steps.
  • Clip Duration and the total at the bottom update as you go. The handles can't cross, and a clip is always at least 0.1 s long.

Reordering

Grab a clip by its ⠿ handle (left of the number) and drag it up or down. A green line shows where it will drop; the card you're moving turns faint.

Only the ⠿ handle picks a card up, so dragging the sliders never moves clips by accident. With the keyboard, tab to the ⠿ handle and press ↑ / ↓.

Using a song more than once

A mashup can come back to a song — for example open with a song's intro and close with its final chorus.

  • ⧉ on a clip card adds another clip of the same song right after it, starting where that clip ends. Drag it wherever you want and trim it.
  • Clicking + Add on a song that's already in the mashup adds a fresh full-length copy.

Removing a clip

Click ✕ on the card. (If the clip came from 🎲 Generate, the song is also remembered as one you don't want — see Your edits & regenerating.)

Name, crossfade & max length

SettingWhat it does
NameThe name of the mashup. Used for saving and for the files you build (letters, numbers, spaces, - and _).
Crossfade (ms)How long each song overlaps the next, in milliseconds (1000 = 1 second). The music fades from one song to the next, and both songs' lights play during the overlap. 0 = straight cut. ✨ Automate can choose this for you.
Max lengthThe longest you want the whole mashup to be — type 5:00, or just 5 for five minutes. Leave empty for no limit. Automate and Generate always stay under it.

Saving & loading

  • 💾 Save (or Ctrl+S) saves the mashup under its Name: every clip and its trim, the order, crossfade, max length and build options.
  • Next to Mashup Timeline you'll see ● unsaved changes or ✓ saved.
  • 📂 Load lists your saved mashups, newest first, with their songs. Click Load to open one or 🗑 to delete it.
  • ➕ New clears the timeline to start again.
The app asks before you lose unsaved work — when loading, starting a new mashup, or closing the tab. Saving under a name that's already used asks before overwriting.
If a song in a saved mashup has since been moved or deleted, the rest still loads and the app lists what's missing.

✨ Automate

Automate works on the songs already in your timeline. It decides which part of each song to use and, if you like, what order to play them in. Nothing changes until you click Apply.

How it decides

  • Lyrics — it reads each sequence's own lyric timing track (the one you or the sequence vendor made in xLights). Lines that repeat are treated as the chorus; lines containing the song's title are the hook. If a sequence has no lyric track, it can look the lyrics up online.
  • Beats — it uses your sequence's Beats / Bars timing tracks, or listens for the beat itself, so every cut lands on a bar line.
  • Never mid-word — a clip starts just before a sung line and ends just after one. It avoids cutting while someone is singing.
  • Flow — it compares tempo, musical key and loudness so each song leads naturally into the next.
OptionWhat it does
Length per songRoughly how long each clip should be (10–90 s). Clips can run a little longer or shorter so they start and end at natural points.
Max mashup lengthSame as the Max length box at the top. If the songs won't fit, clips are shortened first; any song that still can't fit is listed in the preview.
Running orderSee Running order below.

Running order

ChoiceWhat happens
🎯 Best flowThe app picks the order so beats, tempo, key and energy flow from one song into the next. It also likes to open on a song's real intro and close on a song's real ending.
🔀 ScrambleA random order, different every time. It won't give you back your current order or put the same song twice in a row. Cut points are still chosen musically.
📌 Keep my orderYour order stays as it is; only the cut points are chosen.

Reading the preview

After ✨ Analyze (a few seconds — the first time for each song takes a little longer) you see the suggested mashup:

  • Top line — number of clips, total length (against your max), the crossfade, how the order was chosen, and any return visits.
  • Each clip — the times used, the section (Verse 1 → Chorus 1), the first and last lyric line, and the song's tempo (BPM) and key.
  • Between clips — how well the two songs fit: smooth (close tempo, compatible key), good, or bold (a noticeable change — fine for contrast, just less seamless).
  • ⚠ cut overlaps a sung word — there was no clean gap, so the cut touches a word. Worth a quick listen; nudge the clip's handle if needed.
  • The lyric and beat sources at the bottom of each clip tell you what the decision was based on.

Click ✓ Apply to timeline to use it, ⚙ Change options to try different settings, or Cancel to leave your timeline as it was.

🎲 Generate

Generate picks the songs for you. Choose what kind of mashup you want — a theme, genre or mood — and how many songs, and it chooses songs that work together (similar tempo, compatible keys, similar bass and loudness), then arranges them just like ✨ Automate.

First-time scan

The first time you open Generate, the app needs to get to know your library:

  • It listens to every song and reads its lyrics, then sorts the songs into categories.
  • With online lookups on, it takes about 4–5 minutes for ~80 songs (MusicBrainz only allows one request per second). After that, only new or changed songs are checked, so a re-scan takes seconds.
  • You can close the window while it runs — finished songs are saved.
  • Use 🏷 Re-scan library after adding new sequences to your show.

Choosing what to generate

  • Click categories to select them (they turn purple). The number on each is how many songs it has.
  • all = songs must have every selected category (Christmas and Upbeat). any = songs with at least one (Halloween or Comedy). The count next to it shows how many songs match.
  • Number of songs, Length per song, Max mashup length and Let songs come back work as in Automate.
  • With no category selected, any song can be picked (talking intros are left out unless you select Intros & Talking).

Click 🎲 Generate another for a different mashup with the same settings, or ✓ Apply to timeline to use it.

Every generation is different. The app remembers what it has generated and never gives you the same set of songs twice for the same categories. If only a few songs match (e.g. 3 A Cappella songs), it changes the order and the parts used instead.

Your edits & regenerating

Generate respects what you've done to the timeline:

  • 📌 Songs you added yourself (with + Add or by dragging) are always kept.
  • 🚫 Songs you removed from a generated mashup (with ✕) won't be picked again.

So a good way to work is: generate, remove anything you don't want, add any song you do want, then click 🎲 Generate again — it fills the gaps with new songs.

Adding a removed song back with + Add takes it off the "won't be picked" list automatically. Both lists are saved with the mashup.

🔨 Build & download

Click 🔨 Build Mashup. After a few seconds to a minute (depending on length):

📦 Download all (.zip) gives you one zip containing a folder named after your mashup, with the .xsq and .mp3 inside. The files are also kept in the app's output folder.

Even out loudness

Songs are mastered at very different volumes — in this show, Sleigh Ride is almost 8 dB louder than Frosty the Snowman. With Even out loudness ticked (the default), every song is brought to the same loudness, like mp3gain does.

  • The adjustment is worked out from the whole song, so each song keeps its own quiet verses and louder choruses.
  • If a quiet song needs a boost, a limiter stops it from distorting.
  • The build summary shows the adjustment for each clip (e.g. +2.4 dB).

Fade out ending

With Fade out ending ticked (the default, 3 seconds), the mashup fades out instead of stopping abruptly — both the music and the lights.

  • The lights use xLights' own Fade Out transition on the last effects, so you'll see it in xLights' effect settings and can adjust it there.
  • Set the length from 0.5 to 15 seconds in the box next to the checkbox.
If your last clip already ends on the song's real ending (Automate notes say "ends on the song's real ending"), you may prefer to untick the fade so the ending isn't softened.

Videos

Many sequences use the xLights Video effect. Videos aren't cut — instead, when a clip starts part-way through a video, the effect's start time is moved forward so the picture stays in sync with the music. Looping, manual-speed and stretched (Slow/Accelerate) videos are handled too. The build summary says how many videos were re-timed.

After your first few builds, play a section with a trimmed video in xLights to check it starts at the right frame.

Into xLights

  1. Unzip the download into your show's Sequences folder. You'll get a folder named after the mashup containing the .xsq and .mp3.
  2. In xLights, open the .xsq. If xLights asks for the media file, choose the .mp3 in the same folder.
  3. Play it through. Check any spots marked ⚠ cut overlaps a sung word, the transitions, trimmed videos and the ending fade.
  4. Render All (Ctrl+Shift+R) to create the .fseq that your controllers play.
  5. Save, and add the mashup to your playlist or schedule.
Mashups you copy into Sequences show up in the app's library next time too — you can use a mashup as part of another mashup.

Tips & troubleshooting

A song is in the wrong category

Hover over it in the library and click ✎ tags to fix it. Hover over a chip first to see why it was chosen. Automatic tags depend on the lyrics in your sequence and on MusicBrainz recognising the song, so some will need correcting.

The preview says "⚠ cut overlaps a sung word"

There was no clean pause at that point, so the cut touches a word. Listen to it in xLights after building; if it bothers you, nudge that clip's handle a little earlier or later and rebuild.

Transitions are marked "bold"

The two songs have quite different tempos or keys. That's often fine — a change of pace can be fun — but for a smoother mashup choose songs with similar tempos (the BPM is shown in the preview and the category editor), or use 🎲 Generate, which prefers songs that fit together.

Automate or Generate left a song out

All the songs couldn't fit in your Max mashup length even with shorter clips. The preview names the song that was left out. Raise the maximum, lower the number of songs, or shorten Length per song.

The first Generate is slow

The first library scan listens to every song and looks up genres online — about 4–5 minutes for ~80 songs. It only happens once; afterwards only new or changed songs are checked. You can untick the online lookups for a faster scan without genres.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline except the online lyric lookup (only for sequences without a lyric track) and genre lookup. Untick those options; songs then simply won't get genre categories.

A new sequence doesn't appear in the library

The library is read when the app starts. Restart the app after adding sequences to your show, then use 🏷 Re-scan library in the Generate window to categorise them. Sequences inside import folders, and sequences whose music file can't be found, aren't listed.

Some lyric notes look like gibberish ("Fay Lease Nav E Dad")

Some sequences have lyric tracks spelled phonetically to drive singing faces. The app still uses them to find the chorus and the line endings — they just read oddly in the notes.

The app looks out of date after an update

Restart the app (close the window running app.py and start it again), then press Ctrl+F5 in the browser.

Where are my files?
  • Built mashups — output\<name>\ in the app folder (and in your downloads if you used the zip).
  • Saved mashups — saved_mashups\
  • Your category changes — library_overrides.json

Other folders (analysis_cache, library_cache, loudness_cache.json) only speed things up and can be deleted safely.

Glossary

TermMeaning
ClipOne section of one song in the mashup — a card on the timeline.
CrossfadeThe overlap where one song fades out as the next fades in.
BPMBeats per minute — the song's tempo.
Key / Camelot codeThe song's musical key. The Camelot code (e.g. 8A) makes matching easy: the same number, or one apart, usually sounds good together.
Bar line / downbeatThe first beat of a bar — the natural place to start or end a section.
Lyric timing trackThe xLights timing track with the song's words, usually used for singing faces. Automate reads it to find the chorus and where lines end.
LUFSA standard measure of how loud music sounds. The app brings every song to −16 LUFS.
Render / .fseqxLights turns a sequence into a .fseq file of light data. Every new mashup needs rendering once in xLights.
